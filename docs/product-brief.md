# Product brief

Codex Journey is a reflection surface, not a task manager. Its job is to turn scattered work into a defensible narrative: active efforts, evidence-backed outcomes, decisions awaiting the user, three explainable priorities, and one overlooked possibility.

## Principles

1. Proof before progress theater. A successful chat is evidence of activity, never completion.
2. User corrections outrank inference and persist locally.
3. Status vocabulary preserves the gap between implemented, merged, deployed, activated, and verified.
4. Recommendations expose their arithmetic and remain advisory.
5. Private data stays local and exports are redacted.

## Views

Today answers the five core questions. Journey shows time-ordered movement. Projects shows the portfolio. Completed collects implementation-through-verification outcomes. Waiting on Me isolates gates and blockers. Ideas holds non-binding possibilities. Evidence exposes provenance and confidence.
